# Product

## Register

product

## Users

**Players and parents:** Tennis students (teens to adults) and parents who have children in the program. They open the app occasionally — usually after a session — to read coaching notes and check their development plan. Context: relaxed and personal, not a business-tool mindset.

**Coaches (Cooper and Mitch):** Cooper logs session notes after lessons. Mitch maintains the platform. Both access a protected coach dashboard.

## Product Purpose

CTT Academy is a coaching management platform for Cooper Anderson's one-on-one and group tennis coaching business. Cooper logs session notes after each lesson; players access their notes, Long-Term Tennis Development Plan (LTTDP), and coaching history. Success means players check in regularly and Cooper's session logging becomes a natural habit.

## Brand Personality

Warm, personal, credible. Like a note from a coach who knows your name — not a software product, not a generic fitness tracker.

## Anti-references

- Corporate SaaS (Salesforce, HubSpot) — cold, dense, form-heavy, enterprise aesthetic
- Generic fitness trackers (MyFitnessPal, Strava) — blue-gradient athletic tracker look
- Kids' education apps (Duolingo, Khan Academy) — friendly but childish; Cooper's students are teens and adults
- Social media login pages (Instagram) — don't look like a social network sign-in

## Design Principles

1. **Personal over generic.** Every screen should feel made for this specific coaching relationship, not for "tennis coaching software" as a category.
2. **Task disappears.** Players find their notes; Cooper logs his session. No friction, no discovery overhead. The interface fades into the background.
3. **Warmth through specificity.** Warmth doesn't come from decorative elements — it comes from specific, considerate copy and deliberate UI choices that show the product understands its users.
4. **Consistent vocabulary.** Same button shape, same form controls, same color usage across all screens. The brand carries through every detail.
5. **Accessible by default.** WCAG AA throughout. Cooper's student roster spans ages and devices; assume variable visual acuity and screen quality.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA (4.5:1 body text contrast, 3:1 large text). Reduced motion support throughout. Mobile-first; assume iPhone usage as the primary context.
